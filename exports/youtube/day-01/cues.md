# On-screen cue list — Day 1

Title: Zdravo, Sarajevo! / Hello, Sarajevo!
Theme: First walk through Baščaršija

## Vocabulary cards
- zdravo — hello (informal) (ZDRAH-voh)
- dobar dan — good day / hello (polite) (DOH-bar dahn)
- dobro jutro — good morning (DOH-bro YOO-tro)
- dobra večer — good evening (DOH-bra VEH-cher)
- hvala — thank you (HVAH-lah)
- molim — please / you're welcome (MOH-leem)
- doviđenja — goodbye (doh-vee-JEN-ya)
- da — yes (dah)
- ne — no (neh)
- Sarajevo — Sarajevo (sah-rah-YEH-voh)
- Baščaršija — Baščaršija (old bazaar) (bash-CHAR-shi-ya)
- ulica — street (OO-lee-tsa)
- čaj — tea (chigh)
- žena — woman / wife (ZHEH-nah)
- đak — pupil / schoolchild (jak)
- džezva — coffee pot (cezve) (JEZ-vah)

## Dialogue lines
- Ana: Dobar dan!
- Shopkeeper: Dobar dan! Molim?
- Ana: Hvala… čaj?

- Shopkeeper: Doviđenja!
