**Narration:** Sebilj is the wooden fountain in Baščaršija’s main square. Locals say kod Sebilja when they mean meet me at the fountain. Emir is waiting.
**On screen:** Sebilj · Baščaršija · (credit image) ### 3:00 Lesson A. Pronouns and biti
**Narration:** Ja (I. Ti) you. On (he. Ona) she. Now biti. Ja sam, ti si, on je, ona je. Repeat. Ja sam Ana. Ti si Emir. On je vodič.
**On screen (cards):** ja sam | ti si | on je | ona je ### 5:00 Lesson B. Introductions
**Narration:** Say Ja sam Ana, or Zovem se Ana. both work. Add where you are from. Ja sam iz Amerike. When you meet someone. Drago mi je!
**On screen:** Zovem se… · Ja sam iz… · Drago mi je ### 6:30 Mini dialogue
**Narration:** Listen to Ana and Emir at Sebilj. then pause and repeat each line.
